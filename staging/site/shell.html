    How to explore

    Controls

    • Select menu to open all controls. They stay open while you choose. Select Close, press Escape, or select outside the menu to dismiss it.
    • In the constellation, drag or use two-finger trackpad scrolling to move. Use a mouse wheel or pinch to zoom. Select a line or page to focus it; long-press to open it in the journal. Fit whole view shows the full arrangement.
    • In the journal's Read pages mode, swipe horizontally or use the page thumbnails to turn pages. Choose Scan, Text, or Scan & text in Page view. Pinch inward to return to the constellation.
    • Search finds words in the transcription. Choose a result to open it in your current view, or use its Journal or Constellation button to switch views.

    Marks

    • struck = cancelled in the notebook; raised small text = insertion; dotted gap = illegible; dotted underline = uncertain reading.
    • Short rule = passage copied from a published work, not reproduced.

    Arrangements

    • By poem and Scatter are generated anew on each visit; the link keeps the current one. Spiral is notebook order.

    Revisions

    • Step order is an editorial reconstruction from the transcription, not a record of how the page was written. Lines assumed written top to bottom; each cancellation or insertion placed after its stanza.
    • stanza · new draft · revision · order uncertain.
    • Across drafts lines are matched by shared words: = same · ≈ changed · + new · − dropped. Highlighted words are new since the previous draft.

    Hand ↔ Font

    • Each located line crossfades to the same words set in ajp-scribbles, a typeface built from letters on these pages, at the line's measured position and x-height.

    Keyboard

    • While reading: arrows pan or turn pages; + and − zoom; 0 fits the whole view; Enter focuses a line or opens the focused work; [ and ] change versions; B switches between journal and constellation; / opens Search; Space opens the menu.
    • In menus and panels: Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space activates a button, and Escape closes the current panel or menu. Arrow keys adjust sliders and move through search results without moving the reader underneath.
